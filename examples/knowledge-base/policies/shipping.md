# Shipping Information

## Shipping Options

TechGear offers multiple shipping options to meet your needs:

### Standard Shipping
- **Cost**: Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99
- **Delivery Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Available**: All 50 US states

### Express Shipping
- **Cost**: $12.99
- **Delivery Time**: 2-3 business days
- **Available**: Continental US only

### Next-Day Shipping
- **Cost**: $24.99
- **Delivery Time**: 1 business day (order by 2 PM EST)
- **Available**: Select metro areas only

### Same-Day Delivery
- **Cost**: $14.99
- **Delivery Time**: Same day (order by 12 PM local time)
- **Available**: NYC, LA, Chicago, Austin (within 15 miles of store)

## International Shipping

We ship to Canada, UK, and select European countries.

- **Canada**: $19.99, 7-14 business days
- **UK/Europe**: $29.99, 10-21 business days
- Customs and duties may apply

## Order Processing

- Orders are processed within 1 business day
- Cut-off time for same-day processing: 2 PM EST
- Weekend orders are processed on Monday

## Tracking Your Order

1. You'll receive a tracking number via email when your order ships
2. Track your package at techgear.example.com/track
3. Sign up for SMS updates at checkout

## Delivery Issues

### Package Not Received
If tracking shows delivered but you haven't received your package:
1. Check with neighbors and building management
2. Wait 24 hours (sometimes tracking updates before actual delivery)
3. Contact us to file a claim

### Damaged During Shipping
- Take photos of the damaged packaging and item
- Contact us within 48 hours
- We'll send a replacement at no charge

## PO Box Deliveries

- Standard shipping: Available to all PO Boxes
- Express/Next-Day: Not available to PO Boxes
- Large items may require street address
