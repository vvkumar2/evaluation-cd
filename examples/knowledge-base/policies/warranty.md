# Warranty Information

## Standard Warranty

All products sold by TechGear come with a manufacturer's warranty. The warranty period varies by product category:

### Warranty Periods

| Product Category | Warranty Period |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Laptops & Computers | 1 year |
| Smartphones | 1 year |
| Tablets | 1 year |
| Headphones & Audio | 1 year |
| Smart Home Devices | 2 years |
| Gaming Consoles | 1 year |
| Accessories | 90 days |

## What's Covered

The standard warranty covers:
- Manufacturing defects
- Hardware malfunctions under normal use
- Battery defects (capacity below 80% within warranty period)

## What's NOT Covered

The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage (drops, spills, cracks)
- Water damage
- Damage from unauthorized repairs
- Normal wear and tear
- Software issues
- Lost or stolen items

## Filing a Warranty Claim

1. Visit techgear.example.com/warranty
2. Enter your product serial number
3. Describe the issue
4. We'll provide repair or replacement options
5. Free shipping both ways for warranty repairs

## Repair vs Replacement

- We'll attempt repair first
- If unrepairable, you'll receive:
  - Same model replacement (if available)
  - Equivalent or better model
  - Store credit for discontinued items

## Out-of-Warranty Service

For products outside warranty:
- Diagnostic fee: $29.99 (waived if you proceed with repair)
- Repair costs vary by issue
- Get a quote before we begin work
