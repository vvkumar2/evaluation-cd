# Return Policy

## Standard Returns

TechGear offers a **30-day return policy** for most products purchased from our store.

### Requirements for Returns

1. Items must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date
2. Products must be in original packaging with all accessories included
3. Items must be in new or like-new condition
4. Proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation) is required

### How to Return an Item

1. Log into your TechGear account
2. Go to "Order History" and select the order
3. Click "Return Item" and select the reason
4. Print the prepaid return label
5. Pack the item securely and attach the label
6. Drop off at any UPS location

### Refund Processing

- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item
- Original payment method will be credited
- Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

## Exceptions

The following items have modified return policies:

### Non-Returnable Items
- Opened software and digital downloads
- Gift cards
- Custom-built or personalized items
- Items marked as "Final Sale"

### 15-Day Return Window
- Drones and remote-controlled devices
- Opened headphones and earbuds (hygiene reasons)

### Extended Holiday Returns
Items purchased between November 15 and December 31 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:
1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
2. Do not discard the packaging
3. We'll arrange free pickup and replacement
4. Expedited shipping for replacements at no extra charge

## Store Credit Option

Instead of a refund to your original payment method, you can choose store credit which:
- Never expires
- Includes a 10% bonus (e.g., $100 refund = $110 store credit)
- Can be used on any purchase
