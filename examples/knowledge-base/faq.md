# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders & Payments

### How do I track my order?
You can track your order by logging into your TechGear account and visiting "Order History", or use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.

### Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placing them. After that, please wait for delivery and initiate a return.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, TechGear Gift Cards, and Affirm financing for orders over $200.

### Is financing available?
Yes! Orders over $200 qualify for Affirm financing with 0% APR for 6 months for qualified customers.

## Shipping

### How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Otherwise, it's $5.99. Express shipping is $12.99 and next-day is $24.99.

### Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to Canada ($19.99), UK, and select European countries ($29.99). Customs duties may apply.

### How long will my order take to arrive?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express is 2-3 days. Next-day delivery is available for orders placed by 2 PM EST.

## Returns & Refunds

### What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be in original packaging with all accessories.

### How long do refunds take?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

### Can I return opened headphones?
Opened headphones can be returned within 15 days (not 30 days) due to hygiene reasons.

## Account & Security

### How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within minutes.

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption and never store your full credit card number.

### How do I delete my account?
Contact customer support to request account deletion. Your data will be removed within 30 days.
