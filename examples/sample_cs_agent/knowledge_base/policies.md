# TechGear Customer Service Policies

## Refund Policy

### Refund Windows by Membership Tier
- **Standard Members**: 30-day return window from delivery date
- **Gold Members**: 60-day return window from delivery date
- **Platinum Members**: 90-day return window from delivery date

### Refund Approval Limits
- Refunds up to $200: Automatically approved by customer service
- Refunds $200-$1,000: Require manager approval (1-2 business days)
- Refunds over $1,000: Require executive approval (3-5 business days)

### Special Cases
- **Damaged Items**: Full refund regardless of time window, no questions asked
- **Defective Products**: Full refund or replacement within warranty period
- **Wrong Item Shipped**: Full refund plus free return shipping

### Refund Processing Time
- Standard refunds: 3-5 business days to original payment method
- Expedited refunds (Platinum members only): 1-2 business days

### Return Requirements
- Original packaging required for non-damaged returns
- Product must be unused and in resale condition
- Return shipping label provided for all approved returns

## Shipping Policy

### Shipping Speeds
- **Standard Shipping**: 5-7 business days - $5.99
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2-3 business days - $12.99
- **Express Shipping**: 1 business day - $24.99

### Free Shipping
- Orders over $50: Free standard shipping
- Gold Members: Free expedited shipping on all orders
- Platinum Members: Free express shipping on all orders

### Tracking
- All orders include tracking number
- Tracking updates within 24 hours of shipment
- Email notifications for order status changes

## Order Management

### Order Cancellation
- Orders can be cancelled while in "Pending" or "Processing" status
- Once shipped, orders cannot be cancelled (must be returned)
- Cancellation refunds processed within 24 hours

### Order Modification
- Address changes: Only while order is in "Pending" status
- Item changes: Only while order is in "Pending" status
- Shipping speed changes: Until order ships ($10 fee for upgrades)

### Order Tracking
- Available 24 hours after order placement
- Real-time updates during shipping
- Delivery confirmation with signature required for orders over $500

## Customer Service Guidelines

### Response Standards
- **Empathy First**: Always acknowledge customer feelings
- **Solution Focused**: Offer clear next steps
- **Policy Compliant**: Follow all company policies
- **Timely**: Respond within 2 minutes

### Escalation Procedures
- Customer requests manager: Transfer immediately
- Policy exception needed: Escalate to manager
- Abusive behavior: Follow de-escalation protocol, escalate if needed

### Tone Guidelines
- Professional but friendly
- Patient and understanding
- Avoid technical jargon
- Use customer's name when known
